# MadWorld Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-08  
**Service:** MadWorld, a persistent online sandbox MMO for Android  
**Operator / privacy contact:** JoTalbot  
**Contact:** jo.talbot@gmail.com

> This document describes the intended production privacy practices of MadWorld. It is not legal advice. The operator should obtain any jurisdiction-specific legal review required before public distribution.

## 1. Information processed

Depending on features used, MadWorld may process account and authentication identifiers, persistent gameplay state, security and audit records, operational technical information in ordinary server logs, and information sent to the operator for support or account requests.

MadWorld does not require third-party advertising or third-party analytics for this release. Push/Firebase/FCM is outside the release scope.

## 2. Purposes

Information is processed to authenticate players, provide persistent gameplay, prevent abuse and cheating, maintain backups and service reliability, provide support, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Sharing

MadWorld does not sell personal information and does not share information with advertisers for behavioral advertising. Operational infrastructure providers may process information on behalf of the service where necessary to host, secure, monitor, or deliver the service.

## 4. Retention

Gameplay data is retained while an account is active or while needed to provide the persistent game. Security, audit, backup, and legally required records may be retained for a limited additional period when necessary for security, recovery, disputes, or legal compliance.

## 5. Account and data deletion

A player may request deletion by contacting **jo.talbot@gmail.com**. The operator will verify control of the account, then delete or anonymize personal and gameplay data that can be deleted without undermining security, fraud prevention, legal retention, or backup-recovery obligations. Data that must temporarily remain for those purposes will be deleted or anonymized when the applicable retention period ends.

## 6. Security

MadWorld uses server-authoritative mutations, transactional persistence, authentication, idempotency controls, audit records, backups, and operational access controls appropriate to its architecture. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be perfectly secure.

## 7. Children's privacy

MadWorld is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Applicable age and distribution requirements must be followed. Contact the privacy address if information from a child may have been collected unlawfully.

## 8. International processing

The service may use infrastructure in countries other than the player's country. Applicable data-transfer requirements must be assessed for the final production deployment.

## 9. Requests and changes

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other treatment of personal information. Requests may be sent to **jo.talbot@gmail.com**. Material revisions will update the effective date above.
