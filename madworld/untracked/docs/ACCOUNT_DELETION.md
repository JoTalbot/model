# MadWorld Account Deletion

## User request

Send an account-deletion request to **jo.talbot@gmail.com**. Include enough information for the operator to verify control of the account. Do not send passwords, private keys, access tokens, or other secrets by email.

## Operator procedure

1. Verify control of the account using the existing account/support verification process.
2. Identify the player's persistent account and associated gameplay records.
3. Disable access while the deletion operation is processed.
4. Delete or anonymize personal and gameplay records that are eligible for deletion.
5. Remove or anonymize associated support data where applicable.
6. Preserve only records that must temporarily remain for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, incident investigation, or backup recovery.
7. Record completion without retaining unnecessary personal information in the deletion ticket.
8. Confirm completion to the requester.

## Backups

Deletion from the active database does not require destructive rewriting of historical backups. Backups remain subject to the documented retention schedule and are not restored into active service except for approved recovery procedures. When a backup expires under the retention policy, deleted data contained only in that backup ceases to be retained.

## Important limitation

This repository documents the operational deletion process. It does not claim that every jurisdiction's legal requirements are satisfied without the operator's final legal/privacy review.
